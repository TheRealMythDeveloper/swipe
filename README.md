# SWIPE
### THIS IS A GAME OF SPEED AND PRECISION.

SWIPE is a fast-paced arcade challenge built around reaction time, accuracy, memory, and coordination. Complete a collection of mini-games, survive increasingly difficult challenges, and see how long you can last.



## 17 Game Modes!!!

**A Campaign**
Progress through a series of levels, each containing difficult challenges. Complete every challenge to advance.

**Endless**
Keep playing for as long as you can. Test your skills, survive the challenges, and push your score as far as possible.

---

## What Challenges?

SWIPE features a collection of mini-games built around different skills:

- **Slice** - React and slice through targets.
- **Direction** - Follow directional commands.
- **Trace** - Follow paths with precision.
- **Stop** - Stop at the right moment.
- **Deflect** - Redirect incoming objects.
- **Sort** - Sort objects correctly.
- **Flick** - Flick targets with accuracy.
- **Circle** - Complete circular movements.
- **Dodge** - Avoid incoming hazards.
- **Catch** - Catch targets before they disappear.
- **Tap** - Hit targets before time runs out.
- **Memory** - Remember and repeat patterns.
- **Connect** - Connect points in the correct sequence.
- **Balance** - Maintain stability.
- **React** - Respond to sudden changes.
- **Precision** - Hit targets accurately.
- **Follow** - Track and follow moving objects.
- **Combo** - Complete sequences of actions.

Simple names? Yes, But also fun gameplay!
---

## Gameplay Sneak-Peek

- Complete challenges before the timer expires.
- Earn points for successful challenges.
- Difficulty increases as you progress.
- Failure ends your run.
- Test your skills across different types of challenges.
